For the teacher

Quiz Maker

Put in a quiz, download one file, upload it to your repository. The timer, shuffled order, leave detection and sending scores to your Google Form are added for you.

1. Your Google Form

How to connect a form (once per teacher)
  1. Create a Google Form with four questions, in any order: Nama, Kelas, Score (short answer) and Note (paragraph).
  2. In the form's Settings, make sure it does not collect email addresses and does not require sign-in. Do not add other required questions.
  3. Open the three-dot menu at the top right of the form editor and choose Get pre-filled link.
  4. Type NAMA in the name question, KELAS in the class question, SCORE in the score question and NOTE in the note question.
  5. Press Get link, then Copy link, and paste it below.

2. Paste a whole quiz (optional)

If you already have the quiz written as text, paste it here and press the button. The boxes in steps 3 and 4 are then filled in for you. Skip this step if you want to type the quiz into those boxes yourself.
